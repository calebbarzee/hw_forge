# GX12 and GX16 aviation connectors: off-board part, panel numbers

Rules: `skills/hw-design/references/interfaces.md` section 11.

## There is no stock footprint

A GX connector is a panel-mount part with solder cups. The board never carries it. The
packages.json entries `GX12-offboard` and `GX16-offboard` have `pad_count` 0, so a
footprint with pads declared as one is a `kicad_fpcheck.py` FAIL, and a footprint with no
pads reports SKIP. The mic buffer board lands the GX16-4 pigtail on
`Connector_Wire.pretty/SolderWire-0.5sqmm_1x04_P4.6mm_D0.9mm_OD2.1mm`.

## GX16 numbers (secondary: the project's record of the vendor datasheet)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Panel thread | M16 x 1 |
| Panel nut | hexagon, 19 mm across flats |
| Coupling ring thread | M16 x 0.75 |
| Male connector length | 35.5 mm |
| Maximum body diameter | 18.3 mm |
| Solder cup | 0.8 mm aperture, cable up to 5.0 mm |
| Rating | 7 A at 125 V (4 pin) |
| Anti-rotation flat | none on the drawing read (some vendors cut one) |
| Panel hole | not stated: banded 16.2 to 17.0 mm, 16.5 mm used. needs-verification |

## mating_direction

Not a footprint axis: the connector axis is the case wall's normal. State it in board
axes in the project, for example "tail wall, outward". needs-verification for the
specific part: record the direction when the part is placed.

## Mated plug envelope

The mated pair is longer than the 35.5 mm male body (needs-verification: the female cable
plug length was not read). The coupling ring needs a hand: reserve more than the 18.3 mm
body diameter.

## GX12

No GX12 drawing was read. Every GX12 number is needs-verification: thread (M12 x 1 by the
name), nut size, length, rating, panel hole.

## Traps

- Panel hole 16 mm is too small for the M16 thread. Drill 16.2 to 17.0 mm and verify
  against the purchased part before cutting a case.
- A round hole lets a part without a flat spin. Check the part for flats.
- Cup numbering is read from the stamped number, mirrored from the solder side.

## What a research pass must confirm

1. A GX12 manufacturer drawing. 2. The GX16 panel hole and any flat. 3. The female
plug's length and the mated envelope.
