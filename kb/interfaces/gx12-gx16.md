# GX12 and GX16 aviation connectors: off-board part, panel numbers

Rules: `skills/hw-design/references/interfaces.md` section 11.

Correction, 2026-10-03: the earlier version of this card took the Handson
drawing's top view (35.5 mm long, 18.3 mm ring) as the male panel socket and
reversed the thread assignment. Both were wrong; the table below replaces them.
The 35.5 mm and 18.3 mm numbers belong to the female cable plug.

## Two lineages, one reading of the evidence

| Source | Lineage | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| Handson Technology GX16 datasheet (CON1178), p1-p2 | drawing, also copied by Renhotec and AST3D, so those add no independent reading | socket and plug dimensions, thread labels, cup aperture, rating |
| Olimex `gx-connector-dimension.jpg` (CONNECTOR-GX16-4) | independent drawing | mated length, flange, tails, nut |
| Renhotec `GX16-A-4-S.pdf` (plug sheet) | secondary | confirms the Handson top drawing is the straight plug (cable clamp, 6.5 mm bore) |

Where the two drawings differ, both are shown and the band is kept. A swap
between Handson and Olimex parts moves these numbers inside the bands.

## There is no stock footprint

A GX connector is a panel-mount part with solder cups. The board never carries it. The
packages.json entries `GX12-offboard` and `GX16-offboard` have `pad_count` 0, so a
footprint with pads declared as one is a `kicad_fpcheck.py` FAIL, and a footprint with no
pads reports SKIP. The mic buffer board lands the GX16-4 pigtail on
`Connector_Wire.pretty/SolderWire-0.5sqmm_1x04_P4.6mm_D0.9mm_OD2.1mm`. The stock
`SolderWire-*` footprints name a `Connector_Wire.3dshapes` model that does not exist in
the KiCad 10.0.5 install or upstream (dangling link).

## GX16 panel socket (male, solder cup, goes in the wall)

| Item | Value | Band | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|

| Flange OD | 19 mm | none | Handson p2, dimensioned |
| Flange thickness | 1.8 mm | none | Olimex dimensioned, Handson scaled, agree |
| Hex nut across flats | 19.0 (Handson "S19") or 18.8 (Olimex) | 18.8 to 19.0 | dimensioned, both; use about 19 |
| Hex nut across corners | 21.7 to 21.9 mm | derived from across flats | derived |
| Hex nut thickness | 3.4 (Handson) or 2.7 (Olimex) | 2.5 to 3.5 | scaled, neither dimensions it |
| Barrel in front of flange | 5.0 (Handson) or 5.2 (Olimex) | 5.0 to 5.2 | dimensioned, derived |
| Threaded body behind flange, tails excluded | 8.6 (Handson) or 9.1 (Olimex, 13.1 minus 4.0) | 8.6 to 9.1 | dimensioned, derived |
| Solder cup or tail beyond the thread | 5 (Handson) or 4.0 (Olimex) | 4.0 to 5.0 | dimensioned |
| Interior depth behind the flange face, tails included | 13.1 (Olimex) or 13.6 (Handson, 8.6 + 5) | 13.1 to 13.6 | dimensioned, derived: the depth the case must clear |
| Socket body, tails excluded | 15.6 mm (Handson) | 15.4 to 15.6 | Handson, dimensioned. Not 35.5 mm. The rows above sum to 15.4 (5.0 + 1.8 + 8.6); see the note below |
| Panel thickness the thread accepts | about 5 mm maximum, 2 to 4 mm typical | keep the boss wall 2 to 4 mm | derived (thread minus nut); a Verchil guide says "3 to 4 mm engagement" (secondary) |
| Anti-rotation flat | none seen | keep a plain round hole | Handson and Olimex end views and both Olimex photos show a round thread. One blog mentions "a flat area on top" without saying where; a flat on some variants is not excluded |
| Contacts | 4, square layout, one key notch | see cup numbering | Renhotec diagram, single source |
| Solder cup aperture | 0.8 mm | none | Handson p1 |
| Rating | 7 A at 125 V (4 pin) | none | Handson p1 |

Socket body, 15.6 against 15.4 mm: Handson dimensions the 15.6 mm body, 5.0 mm barrel
and 8.6 mm thread. It does not dimension the flange; the 1.8 mm is Olimex's number, and
Handson's flange is only scaled, about 0.2 mm accurate. The recorded sources do not
resolve the 0.2 mm gap. Design to 15.6 mm: it is the larger number and the only
dimensioned total.

Frontmatter confidence is needs-verification because two load-bearing claims are
unverified: the 16.5 mm panel hole and the thread assignment, which rests on one drawing
lineage.

### Threads (conflict C2, reported, not resolved)

| Part | Thread | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Female plug, coupling ring | M16 x 1 | Handson drawing label |
| Socket, front (mating) barrel | M16 x 1 | Handson drawing label |
| Socket, rear (panel) thread | M16 x 0.75 | Handson drawing label |

This reverses the earlier card, which gave panel thread M16 x 1 and coupling ring
M16 x 0.75. The Olimex drawing prints "M16.00*1" once without saying which section.
One drawing lineage carries the assignment, so it stays researched, not verified.
It does not change the hole or the nut (both ship with the part). It matters only
when a case cuts its own thread.

## Mated envelope

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Flange front face to cable clamp end | 30.5 to 30.8 mm | derived from two lineages (Olimex 45.7 minus 14.9; Handson 35.5 minus 5.0 engaged), agree within 0.3 mm |

Finger clearance around the 18.3 mm ring is in no source. 8 mm of free radius beyond
the ring OD is a recommendation, not a sourced number (needs-verification).

## mating_direction

Not a footprint axis: the connector axis is the case wall's normal. State it in board
axes in the project, for example "tail wall, outward". Record the direction when the
part is placed.

## GX12

No GX12 drawing was read. Every GX12 number is needs-verification: thread (M12 x 1 by the
name), nut size, length, rating, panel hole.

## Traps

- Panel hole 16 mm is too small for the M16 thread. Drill 16.2 to 17.0 mm (16.5 used) and
  verify against the purchased part before cutting a case. Do not take a hole size from a
  vendor's marketing text: one guide gives 15.5 to 16.0 mm, which cannot pass a thread
  whose major diameter reaches 16.0.
- Plug dimensions are not socket dimensions. 35.5 mm length and the 18.3 mm ring belong
  to the cable plug outside the case. Inside the case the socket needs 13.1 to 13.6 mm
  behind the panel and about 19 mm across the nut flats.
- A round hole lets a part without a flat spin. Check the part for flats.
- Cup numbering: the Renhotec 4P diagrams number 1 top-left, 2 bottom-left, 3 bottom-right,
  4 top-right for the socket with the key at the top, and the mirror image for the plug.
  Neither diagram says which side it views, and the digits stamped on the cups were not
  legible in any photo. Single source: read the stamp on the purchased part.
- Stock `Connector_Wire` footprints link a 3D model that does not exist.

## What a research pass must confirm

1. A GX12 manufacturer drawing.
2. The GX16 panel hole that passes the thread with the printed wall (16.5 mm assumed), nut
   thickness, thread length behind the flange, tail length, and whether the socket has a flat.
3. The cup numbering as stamped, and which section carries M16 x 0.75.
4. Finger clearance and cable bend radius for the mated plug.
