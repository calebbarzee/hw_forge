## Wuerth 7499010001A (datasheet, read)

Body 16.1 mm wide, 13.55 mm tall, 25.4 mm long. Recommended panel cutout 16.6 x 14.05
mm with a 2.03 mm flange. Hole pattern: 8 x 0.90 mm, 2 x 1.6 mm, 2 x 3.25 mm. Pins: 1 TD+,
2 centre tap, 3 TD-, 4 RD+, 5 centre tap, 6 RD-, 8 chassis (Bob Smith capacitor return).
Inside: 1:1 +-2 % transformers, 350 uH minimum, two 75 ohm resistors per pair to a 1 nF
2 kV capacitor, insulation 2 250 V DC, return loss 18 dB (1 to 30 MHz), 750 mating
cycles, IEEE 802.3u.

## Mated plug envelope and panel opening

Panel cutout for the Wuerth part: 16.6 x 14.05 mm. A plug's boot and latch tab add
height above the cutout (about 1 mm, needs-verification). Plug width at the standard
modular plug is about 11.7 mm (needs-verification).

## Traps

- Wiring a non-magnetic jack straight to a PHY has no isolation.
- Centre-tap bias comes from the PHY datasheet.
- Shield and the Bob Smith return go to chassis through a stated network.
- Leave the two non-plated locating holes in.

## What a research pass must confirm

1. HALO and Amphenol directions from their drawings. 2. IEEE 802.3 isolation clause.
